# Read as a skeptical user
Look only at what is currently visible. Could a reader mistake an estimate for a guarantee, a partial test for production acceptance, a correlation for causation, a subtitle for listened audio, or absence of a report for zero?

Try to reach the evidence for every decisive conclusion. Look for omitted prerequisites, contradictory measurements, old versions, mismatched denominators and hidden qualifiers. Check whether two nodes say the same thing or whether a "resource" is merely decorative.

Return a short defect list with affected nodes, evidence and a concrete fix. Do not supply hidden chain-of-thought. Repair major defects before delivery; leave unresolved evidence gaps explicit instead of polishing them into confidence.
